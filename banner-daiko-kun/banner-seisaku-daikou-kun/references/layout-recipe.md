Use these measurements for `assets/reference-banner.png`. Coordinates are Canva design pixels on a 540 × 959 px canvas. Treat them as a calibrated starting point and visually correct font-metric differences.

## Palette

- Primary red: `#BC0000`
- Black: `#000000`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Pale diagonal gray: `#F4F4F4`
- Book side strip: `#D8CFB7`

## Images

- Cover: width 262, X 22, Y 329. Preserve aspect ratio.

## Decorative lines

## Final comparison priorities

1. Main headline scale and line breaks
2. Cover/portrait balance and overlap
3. CTA clarity
4. Baselines of the two description lines
5. Decorative line placement

The reference book has a stronger three-dimensional perspective than a flat cover plus side strip. Prefer a clean native book mockup when Canva offers one without introducing an unwanted background.
